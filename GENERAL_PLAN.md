# Iron Front: the General (plan)

Asset Builder Chat, 2026-11-10. **Nothing is built until the user says go.**

He's an original, made-up strongman. He is not a likeness of any real person.

## The user's answers
- **Style:** a mix.
  - From the grand generalissimo: a big peaked cap with a large badge, gold-braided epaulettes, a chest of medals and a sash, a greatcoat worn over the shoulders, a swagger stick.
  - From the stern iron man: a heavy moustache, a hard face, a stiff bearing.
- **Colours:** our army olive, dressed up: a darker greatcoat, red collar tabs and gold braid.
- **Role:** a hero unit on the field, walking with bodyguards.
- **Powers** (built later; this round only gives them their poses):
  - **Rally aura:** a raised fist and an arm sweep.
  - **Call in fire:** he either fires a flare pistol at the spot, or barks the order at the nearest comms unit (pointing, a hand cupped to his mouth, shouting at the signaller).
  - **No retreat:** a planted stance, the stick pointing forward.
- **Moves:**
  - an idle that inspects with his hands behind his back;
  - a stiff parade walk;
  - a speech (fist on the podium, pointing, the arm sweeping);
  - taking a salute;
  - the power poses above;
  - wounded: he goes down, two guards carry him off, and he recovers at the HQ sick bay like the officer.
- **Bodyguards:** 2-4 of our approved riflemen, smartened up, with white belts, armbands and fixed bayonets. They walk in escort around him and carry him when he's wounded.
- **Props:**
  - an open staff car he stands up in;
  - a podium with banners.
- **Run:** all at once. Model, moves and props are built together, and you get one look: stills plus frames from the clips.

## How
- **Model:** built on the approved rifleman rig and body (the Soldier, read only). His own kit (cap, coat, medals, stick, flare pistol) goes in my own new files.
- **Moves:** come from the existing body clips plus new pose specs in my own files.
- **Guards:** use the approved rifleman look with the extra kit on top.
- **Car:** the HQ staff car gets reused and dressed up.
- **My paths:**
  - scripts/units/general/
  - tools/gen_*.gd
  - results/general/
  - GENERAL_PLAN.md and GENERAL_REPORT.md
